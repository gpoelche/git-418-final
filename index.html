Summoner's Rift (Draft Pick)

The classic League of Legends experience. Battle on a map with three major lanes, conecting its two sides by a river splitting the jungle. Players queue in teams of five against another team in a fight to destroy the enemy team's base. Various jungle monsters and objectives lie across the map offering benefits to those who smite them. All ranked modes as well as Clash, an elimination-style tournament mode, are played on this map.

Howling Abyss (ARAM)

Looking to unwind and play a fun mode with your friends or alone? Howling Abyss is a map featuring one lane with the pure intent of fighting and moving down one lane to destroy the enemy team's base. All players are offered a pool of randomly-selected champions to play in each game, acting as a mode dedicated entirely to rapid decision making and chaos. The gamemode Mayhem, a seasonal mode providing more chaos with new items and augments to change your playstyle, is also played on Howling Abyss.

Rings of Wrath (Arena)

For some players, showing their prowess of the champions they love is what they love doing most. Rings of Wrath is home to mode Arena, a gamemode dedicated entirely to winning combat against teams of two players. Players pick powerful augments to elevate their champion's capabilities and purchase items to become as strong as possible. Teams are eliminated by depleting their team's health points, with the last team standing being the winner. This is the perfect mode for those looking to flex their skills with their favorite champion either alongside a friend or with a stranger.
